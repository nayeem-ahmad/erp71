# CRM activity unification — design

**Date:** 2026-08-12
**Scope:** merge `LeadConversation`, `CustomerInteraction`, `CrmFollowUp` and the three
`Lead.next_step_*` columns into one `CrmActivity` model, polymorphic over lead and customer,
where a status field — not a table — separates the planned from the logged.

---

## Problem

CRM currently answers "what do I owe this person?" from four places that never reconcile.

**`Lead.next_step` / `next_step_date` / `next_step_assigned_to`**
(`packages/database/prisma/schema.prisma:1998-2000`) is a single mutable text field with **no
completion state and no history**. Logging a conversation overwrites it
(`apps/backend/src/crm-lead-conversations/crm-lead-conversations.service.ts:125-131`), so "what did
we promise last week, and did we do it?" is unanswerable.

**`CrmFollowUp`** (`schema.prisma:2267`) is a real task — `due_at`, `status`, `completed_at`,
`assigned_to` — attachable to a lead or a customer, and generated by two nightly crons.

These two overlap and disagree:

- **Two "due today" lists.** The leads page `myActionsToday` filter reads `next_step_assigned_to` +
  `next_step_date` (`apps/backend/src/crm-leads/crm-leads.service.ts:257-258`). The dashboard's only
  due/overdue cards count `CrmFollowUp`
  (`apps/frontend/src/components/dashboard/CrmDashboard.tsx:185-201`). A rep with five next steps due
  today and no follow-up rows sees a clean dashboard.
- **Only one feeds scoring.** `computeLeadScore` penalises an overdue `next_step_date`
  (`apps/backend/src/crm-leads/lead-scoring.util.ts:64`). A follow-up three weeks overdue moves the
  score by zero.
- **Only one notifies, and barely.** `notifyOwner` fires only from the two cron paths; manual
  `create()` notifies nobody. An overdue `next_step` notifies nobody ever.
- **Three owners per lead.** `assigned_to`, `next_step_assigned_to` and `CrmFollowUp.assigned_to`
  can name three different people with no rule reconciling them.
- **Two orderings.** Leads default-order by `next_step_date`; follow-ups order by `due_at`.

**`LeadConversation`** (`schema.prisma:2229`) is the past-tense log — `summary`, `outcome`,
`direction`, and a FK to the tenant-owned `ConversationChannel` (`schema.prisma:2206`). It is not a
duplicate of `CrmFollowUp`; it is the other half of an activity model. But the two are never wired
together:

- **The loop does not close.** Logging a conversation advances `next_step` but never completes the
  follow-up that prompted the call, and never creates the next one. Both widgets sit on the same
  lead detail page and the rep does the work twice by hand.
- **Completion records nothing.** `update()` sets `status: 'DONE'` and stamps `completed_at` — no
  summary, no channel, no outcome, and no `conversation_id` linking the task to whatever satisfied
  it.
- **Completion is invisible.** A logged conversation bumps the lead score and `last_contacted_at`.
  Completing a follow-up moves neither, so the reorder cron — which fires on 60 days of no contact —
  can re-fire at a lead the team called and marked done.

**`CustomerInteraction`** (`schema.prisma:1946`) is field-for-field `LeadConversation` — `type`,
`direction`, `summary`, `outcome`, `created_by`, `created_at`, same indexes — split only by which
entity it hangs off. The two have already drifted: leads got the tenant-owned channel FK, customers
still carry a hardcoded `// CALL | SMS | WHATSAPP | EMAIL | VISIT | NOTE` comment. And `convert()`
copies only name/phone/email/address (`crm-leads.service.ts:640-645`), so the entire pre-sale
conversation history stays pinned to the closed lead and the new customer's log starts empty.

**Lifecycle hole that falls out of the split.** `validateFollowUpTarget` refuses to create follow-ups
on lost or converted leads (`apps/backend/src/crm-follow-ups/crm-follow-ups.service.ts:46`), but
conversion never closes the existing `PENDING` ones. They stay in the overdue count forever, on leads
nobody will ever work again.

### What mature CRMs do instead

Four patterns are near-universal, and this design adopts all four:

1. **One polymorphic activity table**, not one per parent type — Dynamics `ActivityPointer` +
   `regardingobjectid`, Salesforce `Task` + `WhoId`/`WhatId`, HubSpot engagements associated with any
   object. Nobody splits by parent entity.
2. **Plan and log are the same row, separated by status.** Pipedrive: one `Activity` with a `done`
   boolean. Dynamics: `scheduledstart`/`actualstart` + `statecode`. Salesforce: a Task with
   `Status = Completed` *is* the logged call.
3. **"Next step" is a computed rollup, never typed by hand** — HubSpot `hs_next_activity_date`,
   Pipedrive `next_activity_date`, both read-only. The counter-example is Salesforce's free-text
   `Opportunity.NextStep`, which is the field this codebase independently reinvented and which is
   widely replaced in practice because it goes stale.
4. **Completion prompts the next schedule.** Pipedrive's activity-based selling is built on it.

---

## Non-goals

- **Absorbing `ProjectTask` or `ProjectTaskActivity`.** Project work has its own board model. Out of
  scope.
- **Email/SMS campaign sends becoming activities.** `CrmCampaignRecipient` stays as it is; a bulk
  blast is not a per-person activity and would drown the timeline.
- **Recurring or cadence/sequence activities.** A future feature; nothing here blocks it.
- **A calendar view.** The unified list is enough for this release.
- **Reconciling `Lead.assigned_to` with activity assignment.** They stay independent — lead ownership
  and task ownership are legitimately different. Only the `next_step_assigned_to` duplication is
  removed, by making it a rollup.

---

## Decisions

| Decision | Choice | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Plan vs log | One table, `status` separates them | Pipedrive/Dynamics/Salesforce all do this; it is what makes the completion loop expressible |
| Parent | Nullable `lead_id` + `customer_id`, exactly one set | `CrmFollowUp` already enforces this rule; reuse it verbatim |
| Type axes | Two: `channel_id` (how) + `purpose_id` (why) | `BIRTHDAY` is not a channel; flattening them loses "birthday call, by phone" |
| `next_step_*` | Keep the names, demote to read-only rollup | Zero churn on the leads list, sort allowlist, `myActionsToday`, CSV import, scoring, AI data service |
| Rollout | Expand → backfill → contract | Production has no migration runner; see below |
| Dual-write during R1 | **No** | Rollback leaves legacy tables stale, not wrong; see §Rollout |

## Data model

### `CrmActivity`

```prisma
model CrmActivity {
  id           String    @id @default(uuid())
  tenant_id    String
  store_id     String?

  /// Exactly one of lead_id / customer_id is set — the same rule CrmFollowUp
  /// already enforces in validateFollowUpTarget, lifted into the new service.
  lead_id      String?
  customer_id  String?

  /// WHY the activity exists. Tenant-owned taxonomy, seeded General / Collection
  /// / Birthday / Reorder Reminder.
  purpose_id   String?
  /// HOW the person was (or will be) reached. The existing tenant-owned
  /// ConversationChannel. Nullable while PLANNED — a rep scheduling "chase the
  /// invoice" often does not yet know whether it will be a call or a WhatsApp —
  /// and required by the service on completion.
  channel_id   String?
  /// The channel's `code`, denormalised, exactly as LeadConversation.type already
  /// is: every filter and groupBy reads it, and the composite index serves those
  /// directly.
  channel_code String?

  /// The planned title — "Call about the overdue invoice". Null on an activity
  /// logged directly with no prior plan, which genuinely has no separate title.
  subject      String?
  status       String    @default("PLANNED") // PLANNED | DONE | CANCELLED
  due_at       DateTime?
  completed_at DateTime?

  /// What actually happened. Null while PLANNED, required on completion.
  summary      String?
  outcome      String?
  /// Pre-call notes, carried over from CrmFollowUp.notes. Distinct from `summary`:
  /// this is written before, that is written after.
  notes        String?
  direction    String    @default("OUTBOUND") // INBOUND | OUTBOUND

  assigned_to  String?
  created_by   String?
  origin       String    @default("MANUAL") // MANUAL | BIRTHDAY_CRON | REORDER_CRON | IMPORT

  /// Backfill provenance. The sync script runs on every container start, so it
  /// must be re-runnable; the unique index below is what makes it idempotent.
  /// Both columns are dropped in the contract release.
  legacy_source String?  // LEAD_CONVERSATION | CUSTOMER_INTERACTION | CRM_FOLLOW_UP | LEAD_NEXT_STEP
  legacy_id     String?

  created_at   DateTime  @default(now())
  updated_at   DateTime  @updatedAt

  tenant   Tenant              @relation(fields: [tenant_id], references: [id], onDelete: Cascade)
  lead     Lead?               @relation(fields: [lead_id], references: [id], onDelete: Cascade)
  customer Customer?           @relation(fields: [customer_id], references: [id], onDelete: Cascade)
  purpose  CrmActivityPurpose? @relation(fields: [purpose_id], references: [id], onDelete: Restrict)
  channel  ConversationChannel? @relation(fields: [channel_id], references: [id], onDelete: Restrict)
  assignee User?               @relation("CrmActivityAssignee", fields: [assigned_to], references: [id])
  creator  User?               @relation("CrmActivityCreator", fields: [created_by], references: [id])

  @@unique([tenant_id, legacy_source, legacy_id])
  @@index([tenant_id, status, due_at])
  @@index([tenant_id, assigned_to, status, due_at])
  @@index([tenant_id, lead_id, status, due_at])
  @@index([tenant_id, customer_id, status, due_at])
  @@index([tenant_id, status, completed_at])
  @@index([tenant_id, channel_id])
  @@index([tenant_id, purpose_id])
}
```

**Why `subject` and `summary` are both nullable.** They are not two names for one thing. A planned
activity has a subject and no summary; an activity logged directly ("just called them, here is what
they said") has a summary and no meaningful separate title. The service enforces the rule rather than
the column: `subject` required when creating with `status = PLANNED`, `summary` and `channel_id`
required on completion. The UI renders `subject ?? summary`.

**`onDelete: Restrict` on both taxonomies** mirrors `Lead.sourceOption` / `Lead.categoryOption`, so an
orphaned activity is structurally impossible rather than prevented by an app-layer check.

**`@@unique([tenant_id, legacy_source, legacy_id])` does not constrain normal rows.** Every
user-created activity leaves both columns null, and Postgres treats NULLs as distinct — so any number
of them coexist while the index still makes the backfill idempotent. Same reasoning as the
normalise-blank-mobile-to-NULL rule on `CrmContact`.

### `CrmActivityPurpose`

A copy of `ConversationChannel`'s shape — the precedent is deliberate, so the CRM Settings screen can
reuse the taxonomy component it already has.

```prisma
model CrmActivityPurpose {
  id         String   @id @default(uuid())
  tenant_id  String
  code       String
  name       String
  icon       String?
  sort_order Int      @default(0)
  is_system  Boolean  @default(false)
  is_active  Boolean  @default(true)
  created_at DateTime @default(now())
  updated_at DateTime @updatedAt

  tenant     Tenant        @relation(fields: [tenant_id], references: [id], onDelete: Cascade)
  activities CrmActivity[]

  @@unique([tenant_id, code])
  @@unique([tenant_id, name])
  @@index([tenant_id, is_active, sort_order])
}
```

Seeded rows, matching the four values `CrmFollowUp.type` carries today: `GENERAL` (General),
`COLLECTION` (Collection), `BIRTHDAY` (Birthday), `REORDER_REMINDER` (Reorder Reminder).

**`PURPOSE = 'purposes'` joins `LeadTaxonomyKind`** (`apps/backend/src/crm-lead-taxonomy/lead-taxonomy.dto.ts:20-24`)
so the existing taxonomy service, controller, resolver and settings UI all pick it up, and the
existing `sync-lead-taxonomy` script seeds it — no new script and no new link in the Dockerfile
start chain.

**Implementation trap:** `packages/database/package.json` sets `main: ./index.js` / `types: ./index.ts`,
so the seed exists as a `.ts` and a hand-maintained `.js` mirror
(`prisma/lead-taxonomy.seed.ts` and `.js`), with `lead-taxonomy-catalogue.spec.ts` failing on drift.
The purpose defaults must be added to **both** files. A `.ts`-only edit compiles clean and is
`undefined` at runtime.

### Rollup columns

`Lead` keeps `next_step`, `next_step_date`, `next_step_assigned_to` under their current names and
gains `next_activity_id String?`. `Customer` gains `next_activity_id` and `next_activity_date`.

**`Customer.last_contacted_at` already exists** (`schema.prisma:1838`) and is reused rather than
paired with a new `last_activity_date` — adding a second last-touched column would be precisely the
duplication this design removes. It is written today only by
`crm-interactions.service.ts:33`; completion becomes its second writer.

All five become **write-protected**: only `CrmActivityService.recalculateRollup()` writes them,
in the same transaction as every activity mutation, sourced from the earliest `PLANNED` activity for
that parent (`ORDER BY due_at ASC NULLS LAST, created_at ASC`). When no planned activity remains,
all four go null.

The lead's `next_step` therefore becomes the earliest planned activity's `subject`, its
`next_step_date` that activity's `due_at`, and its `next_step_assigned_to` that activity's
`assigned_to`. The leads list columns, the `resolveOrderBy` allowlist, `myActionsToday`, the CSV
importer, `lead-scoring.util.ts` and `ai/chat-data.service.ts:606-625` all keep working unchanged.

`Lead.last_contacted_at` already exists and keeps its meaning; `Customer.last_activity_date` is its
customer-side equivalent.

**Removed write paths.** `next_step` stops being accepted on `PATCH /crm/leads/:id`, on
`POST /crm/lead-conversations`, and as a CSV import column pair. The CSV importer is the one that
needs a decision rather than a deletion: `next_step` / `next_step_date` columns in an uploaded file
now **create a PLANNED activity** for the imported lead rather than writing the column, with
`origin = IMPORT`.

## Backend

New module `apps/backend/src/crm-activities/` — service, controller, dto, module — replacing
`crm-lead-conversations`, `crm-interactions` and `crm-follow-ups` in the contract release.

### Routes

| Method | Path | Permission | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| `POST` | `/crm/activities` | `MANAGE_CRM_TASKS` to plan, `CREATE_CRM_INTERACTIONS` to log | `status` in the body decides which |
| `GET` | `/crm/activities` | `VIEW_CRM_INTERACTIONS` | filters: `leadId`, `customerId`, `target`, `status`, `assignedTo`, `purpose`, `channel`, `dueToday`, `overdue`; `page`/`limit`; `sortBy`/`sortDir` via `resolveOrderBy` |
| `GET` | `/crm/activities/summary` | `VIEW_CRM_INTERACTIONS` | `{ dueToday, overdue, total }`, replaces `getTodaySummary` |
| `GET` | `/crm/activities/:id` | `VIEW_CRM_INTERACTIONS` | |
| `PATCH` | `/crm/activities/:id` | `MANAGE_CRM_TASKS` | reschedule, reassign, edit subject/notes/purpose |
| `POST` | `/crm/activities/:id/complete` | `CREATE_CRM_INTERACTIONS` | the closed loop — see below |
| `POST` | `/crm/activities/:id/cancel` | `MANAGE_CRM_TASKS` | sets `CANCELLED`; never deletes history |
| `DELETE` | `/crm/activities/:id` | `MANAGE_CRM_TASKS` | hard delete, for mistakes only |

The permission vocabulary already exists and already matches this split —
`VIEW_CRM_INTERACTIONS` / `CREATE_CRM_INTERACTIONS` / `MANAGE_CRM_TASKS`
(`packages/shared-types/index.ts:75-77`). No new permissions.

### `POST /crm/activities/:id/complete`

The endpoint the whole design exists for.

```jsonc
{
  "channel": "CALL",                    // required — id or code, resolved like conversations
  "summary": "Spoke to Karim...",       // required
  "outcome": "Promised payment Thursday",
  "direction": "OUTBOUND",
  "next": {                             // optional — the loop
    "subject": "Confirm payment received",
    "due_at": "2026-08-15T10:00:00Z",
    "purpose": "COLLECTION",
    "assigned_to": "<user-id>"
  }
}
```

In **one transaction**: mark the activity `DONE` with `completed_at = now`; stamp
`Lead.last_contacted_at` / `Customer.last_activity_date`; create the `next` activity when present;
recalculate the rollup; rescore the lead. Returns `{ completed, next }`.

Completing an already-`DONE` or `CANCELLED` activity is a 400, not a no-op — a double-submit that
silently succeeded would produce a second "next" activity.

### Lead scoring

`computeLeadScore` is unchanged. Its `conversationCount` input becomes
`count(CrmActivity where lead_id AND status = 'DONE')`. Post-backfill this is numerically identical
to today's `LeadConversation` count, because every conversation backfills to exactly one `DONE`
activity — **no lead is rescored on migration day**, and a test asserts it against a fixture.

Completing an activity now rescores; today only logging a conversation does. This is the intended
fix, not a side effect.

### Crons

Both jobs move to the new service with unchanged schedules, unchanged `JOB_NAMES`, and unchanged
dormancy threshold (`REORDER_DORMANT_DAYS = 60`). They create `PLANNED` activities with
`origin = BIRTHDAY_CRON` / `REORDER_CRON` and `purpose = BIRTHDAY` / `REORDER_REMINDER`.

The reorder cron's dormancy check reads `last_activity_date` / `last_contacted_at`, which completion
now stamps — so it stops re-firing at customers the team actually called.

### Lifecycle fixes

- `convert()` and any transition to `LOST` **cancel all `PLANNED` activities** for that lead and null
  its rollup, inside the existing transaction. The current behaviour leaves them `PENDING` and
  counted as overdue forever.
- `notifyOwner` fires on **any** create with an `assigned_to` that is not the acting user, not just
  cron-created ones. The existing "don't notify the person already looking at the form" guard is
  preserved.

## Frontend

### `CrmActivityPanel`

One component replacing `FollowUpPanel`, the lead detail conversation list, and the customer detail
interaction list. Props: exactly one of `leadId` / `customerId`, matching `FollowUpPanel`'s existing
contract.

Two sections: **Planned** (`due_at` ascending, overdue rows in `red-600` per the semantic colour
rule) and **History** (`completed_at` descending). Header actions: *Schedule* and *Log activity* —
the second creates an already-`DONE` activity in one step, for the call you made before you thought
to plan it.

Completing opens a `ModalShell` with channel select, summary, outcome, then a collapsible
**"Schedule the next one"** block pre-filled with the same purpose and assignee and a date left
blank. Skippable — but present by default, because prompting is the entire point.

### Pages

- `/crm/follow-ups` → `/crm/activities`, with `routes.crm.followUps` kept as a redirect, following
  the `sales/crm/tasks` precedent. Filters gain purpose and channel.
- Lead detail and customer detail each drop two widgets and gain one.
- Leads list unchanged — the rollup columns keep working.
- Dashboard overdue/due-today cards read `/crm/activities/summary`, which now includes what used to
  be `next_step` — closing the gap where next steps were invisible to the dashboard.
- CRM Settings gains an Activity Purposes tab alongside Lead Sources, Categories and Channels,
  reusing the existing taxonomy screen.

All new copy in en/bn/ms, parity-tested. Tenant-entered purpose names render verbatim and are never
translated, matching the rule already established for sources and channels.

## Rollout

Production has **no migration runner**: `apps/backend/Dockerfile:105` runs
`prisma db push --accept-data-loss` on every container start, and
`packages/database/prisma/migrations/` is never executed there. A table merge expressed naively emits
`DROP TABLE` + `CREATE TABLE`, not a data-preserving migration. So this ships in three releases, the
same expand → backfill → contract shape as `docs/crm/lead-taxonomy-rollout.md`.

### R1 — expand

Additive only. `CREATE TABLE CrmActivity`, `CrmActivityPurpose`; `ADD COLUMN` for the four rollup
columns. **Verified, not assumed:** `prisma migrate diff` between the pre- and post-change schema
must emit zero `DROP` and zero `ALTER COLUMN`. The new module ships and becomes the write path;
legacy endpoints stay alive and keep reading their own tables.

`prisma/sync-crm-activities.ts` joins the Dockerfile start chain after `sync:lead-taxonomy` (which
must have seeded the purposes first) and backfills per tenant:

| Source | → `status` | `subject` | `summary` | `due_at` | `completed_at` | `purpose` | `channel` |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `LeadConversation` | `DONE` | — | `summary` | — | `created_at` | — | `channel_id` |
| `CustomerInteraction` | `DONE` | — | `summary` | — | `created_at` | — | resolve `type` → code |
| `CrmFollowUp` `PENDING` | `PLANNED` | `title` | — | `due_at` | — | `type` → code | — |
| `CrmFollowUp` `DONE` | `DONE` | `title` | — | `due_at` | `completed_at` | `type` → code | — |
| `Lead.next_step` | `PLANNED` | `next_step` | — | `next_step_date` | — | `GENERAL` | — |

Details that matter:

- **`CustomerInteraction.type` resolves by a plain code match.** The seeded channel list already
  contains all six values it can hold — `CALL, SMS, WHATSAPP, EMAIL, VISIT, NOTE`
  (`prisma/lead-taxonomy.seed.js:36-48`) — because those codes mirror the old `LeadConversationType`
  enum. No seeding step is needed. A tenant-renamed channel still matches, because the join is on
  `code`, not `name`. An unresolvable value leaves `channel_id` null and is reported, not guessed.
- **`Lead.next_step` is materialised only when the lead has no `PLANNED` activity already due the
  same day.** Otherwise it is the duplicate this whole design exists to remove, and importing it
  would manufacture a second copy of one task. Skipped rows are counted and logged.
- **Leads already `CONVERTED` or `LOST` do not get their `next_step` materialised at all**, and
  their `CrmFollowUp` `PENDING` rows backfill as `CANCELLED` rather than `PLANNED` — otherwise the
  merge would import the stale-overdue bug it is meant to fix.
- **Idempotent by construction** via `@@unique([tenant_id, legacy_source, legacy_id])` and
  `skipDuplicates`. The script runs on every container start; running it twice must produce the same
  row count, and a test asserts exactly that.
- **It warns rather than exiting non-zero** on unresolved rows. It sits in an `&&` chain ahead of
  `node main.js`, where a non-zero exit is a full outage rather than a failed script — the same
  reasoning `sync-lead-taxonomy` documents.

### R2 — cutover

Frontend moves to the new endpoints and `CrmActivityPanel`. Legacy endpoints remain but are
unreferenced.

### R3 — contract

Gated on: legacy row counts matching backfilled counts per tenant, and no legacy-table writes for
seven days. Drops `LeadConversation`, `CustomerInteraction`, `CrmFollowUp`, the two `legacy_*`
columns, and the three legacy backend modules. `Lead.next_step_*` **survive** — they are the rollup
now, not legacy.

### The one deliberate tradeoff: no dual-write in R1

The lead-taxonomy rollout dual-wrote its legacy enum columns because a rollback would have found
leads with a null source — a correctness hole. This case is different in kind: a rollback to R0 code
finds the legacy tables merely **stale**. Activities created during the R1 window live in
`CrmActivity`, are invisible to R0 code, and reappear intact on roll-forward. Nothing is destroyed,
and the rollback window is a container restart.

Mirroring writes into three legacy tables to avoid temporary invisibility is not worth the write-path
complexity it adds to every create and complete. Recorded here because it is the one
reversibility-versus-cost call in this design, and it is the thing to revisit first if R1 goes badly.

## Testing

- **Backfill idempotency** — run the sync twice against a seeded fixture, assert identical row
  counts and no duplicate `legacy_id`.
- **Backfill mapping** — one case per source row in the table above, including the `next_step`
  same-day dedupe skip and the converted/lost lead cancellation.
- **Rollup correctness** — after create, complete, cancel, reschedule, reassign, and delete; and the
  all-null case when no planned activity remains.
- **Complete-with-next atomicity** — a failure creating the `next` activity rolls back the
  completion; a double-complete is a 400.
- **Scoring unchanged** — a fixture lead scores identically before and after backfill.
- **Lifecycle** — converting a lead cancels its planned activities and nulls the rollup; the reorder
  cron does not re-fire at a customer whose activity was completed inside the dormancy window.
- **Exactly-one-parent** — both set and neither set are 400s, on every write endpoint.
- **Tenant scoping** — every endpoint, cross-tenant id returns 404 not 403.
- **Taxonomy** — `.ts`/`.js` seed mirrors agree (extends the existing catalogue drift spec);
  `onDelete: Restrict` blocks deleting a purpose in use.
- **Locale parity** — en/bn/ms.
- **Frontend** — `CrmActivityPanel` renders planned above history, marks overdue, and the completion
  modal's next-activity block is skippable.

## Follow-ups not in this spec

- Recurring activities and multi-step cadences.
- A calendar view over `due_at`.
- Carrying lead activity history onto the customer at conversion — the merged table makes this a
  small change (re-point `lead_id` rows or read through `converted_customer_id`), but it is a
  product decision about whether pre-sale history should appear on the customer timeline.
- Activity-based rotting indicators on the leads list ("no activity scheduled"), the Pipedrive nag.
